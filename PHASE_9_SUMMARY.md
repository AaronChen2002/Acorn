## 🔄 **Category Consolidation**

### **Problem Solved**
- **Before**: 7 categories with some overlap and confusion
- **After**: 5 streamlined categories with clear boundaries

### **Consolidation Changes**
- **"Hobbies" → "Self Care"** - Recreational activities now part of wellness
- **"Networking" → "Side Work"** - Professional connections part of secondary work
- **Reduced from 7 to 5 categories** for better focus and clarity

### **New Category Structure**
| **Category** | **Includes** | **Color** |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Work** | Main job, meetings, professional tasks | Dark Blue |
| **Side Work** | Freelance, consulting, networking, business connections | Blue |
| **Social** | Personal relationships, friends, family | Light Blue |
| **Self Care** | Exercise, hobbies, wellness, creative pursuits, entertainment | Lighter Blue |
| **Other** | Travel, errands, household tasks | Sky Blue |

## 🎨 **Color Scheme Optimization**

### **New Color Spectrum**
- **Work**: `#1e293b` (Dark blue) - Most important activities

- **Self Care**: `#60a5fa` (Lighter blue) - Wellness + hobbies
- **Other**: `#7dd3fc` (Sky blue) - Miscellaneous activities
